Optimum-centred helpers and conflict, in 2D

The 12 optimum-centred helpers on the 11 multimodal BBOB functions (2D, instance 1): Gallagher functions whose global component sits on $x^\star$ of $f$, alone or with 100 random spheres. The third map shows the conflict between $f$ and $h$, measured at each point by the cosine of the angle between their gradients (red: going down in $f$ is going up in $h$), or on the basin $B$ of $x^\star$ and on the rest of the window by Kendall's $\tau$ or Pearson's $r$ between their values. Zoom with the slider or with the mouse wheel over a map; a click on a map moves the centre there.
