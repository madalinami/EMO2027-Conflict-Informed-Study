Helpers and conflict, in 2D

The Gallagher functions: the ten functions of our experiments (function 3 is the one of Fig. 2) and BBOB f21 and f22, each with its helpers. The third map shows the conflict between $f$ and $h$, measured at each point by the cosine of the angle between their gradients, or on each zone of $f$ (where one component is the highest) by Kendall's $\tau$ or Pearson's $r$ between their values. Zoom with the slider or with the mouse wheel over a map; a click on a map moves the centre there.
